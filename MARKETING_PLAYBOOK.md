### Visual Production Rules:
1. **Dark Mode Only:**  
   Always capture screenshots in **Telegram Dark Mode** (pure black `#000000` or deep obsidian `#18222d`). Never use light mode. Dark mode communicates technical sophistication and exclusivity.
2. **Neon Highlighter on Budget Badges:**  
   Use an image markup tool (e.g. Canva, Figma, Apple Photos Markup) to draw a vibrant **neon green (`#00FF66`)** or **electric yellow (`#FFE600`)** pill border or glow around the budget badge (`💰 $8,500 FIXED` or `⏱️ $85/HR`).
3. **Strategic Redaction ("The Psychological Open Loop"):**  
   - **Client/Company Name:** Apply a heavy frosted gaussian blur sticker or a sleek dark box over the company name.
   - **Apply Button:** Replace or overlay the `[🚀 Apply on Source]` button with a high-contrast badge that reads:  
     `🔒 [SUBSCRIBER EXCLUSIVE LINK — JOIN IN BIO]`.
   - **Why this works:** The viewer can verify the budget ($8,500) and the required skills, but their brain craves closure. The only way to access the direct client link is to join your Whop channel.
4. **Clean Cropping & Dimensions:**  
   - **LinkedIn / X (Twitter):** Crop to a crisp 1:1 square (1080x1080px) or 4:5 vertical (1080x1350px).
   - **TikTok / IG Reels / YouTube Shorts:** Crop as full 9:16 vertical (1080x1920px) positioned in the upper two-thirds of the screen to avoid platform UI overlays.
